About

I hold a PhD from IMECC, Unicamp, Brazil. I am a researcher in differential and algebraic geometry.

My work primarily focuses on gauge theory, with a particular interest in variational aspects of the Yang–Mills–Higgs functional.

I also study the construction of special stable vector bundles associated with solutions to the deformed Hermitian–Yang–Mills equation.

Research interests

  • Differential geometry
    Gauge theory, variational calculus and geometric analysis.
  • Algebraic geometry
    Stability conditions and constructions of stable sheaves.
